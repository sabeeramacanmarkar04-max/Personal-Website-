About Me

he human equivalent of a wave that never stops moving

Hey, Im Sabeera!

Im the friend your friends warn you about before bringing me to public places. I talk to every stranger, try everything at least once, and somehow always end up doing something embarrassing… and I wouldnt change a thing.

My heart belongs to two things: the ocean and freedom. Surfing is pure therapy — just me, my soul, and the sea that doesnt care about achievements. When Im not chasing waves, youll find me building RC cars, painting, riding my bike, playing piano, or screaming at the TV during Formula 1 races (Max Verstappen is the GOAT, fight me).

And yes… One Piece is life. Luffy is my ultimate role model. Be free. Be kind. Be ridiculously loud about the things you love.
